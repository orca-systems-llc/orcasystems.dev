We’re an independent software company. We build and support two Mac apps: Smallfry, for software projects run with coding agents, and Tusk, for classic strategy games on Apple silicon.

Products

smallfry.sh macOS

Smallfry

Ship software with coding agents, without managing every step.

Smallfry turns a goal into a plan and runs the work through the agents you already use, such as Codex, Claude Code, and Cursor. It shows you what’s approved, what’s verified, and what needs your decision. Work counts as done only when the evidence checks out and the change is merged.

Visit smallfry.sh
gettusk.app macOS · Apple silicon

Tusk

Classic Windows strategy games, on your Mac.

Tusk lets you play real-time strategy games you already own on Apple silicon. Import your copy and launch it from a native Mac app. We support a short list of titles and get each one right before adding the next, starting with Empire Earth and The Art of Conquest from the GOG Gold Edition.

Visit gettusk.app
