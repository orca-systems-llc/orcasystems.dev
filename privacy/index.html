Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026

This policy explains how Orca Systems LLC (“Orca Systems,” “we,” “us”) handles information in connection with this website and our Mac apps, Smallfry and Tusk.

The short version: we don’t require accounts, we don’t run ads or third-party analytics, and we don’t sell personal information. Your projects, games, and saves stay on your Mac.

Information we collect

This website

orcasystems.dev is a static website. It has no accounts, forms, cookies, analytics, or advertising. Our host, GitHub Pages, automatically receives standard request data such as IP address, browser type, and the page requested, as any web server does. We do not use this data to identify visitors.

Email

If you email us, we receive your email address, any name you provide, and the contents of your message. We use this only to respond to you.

Smallfry

  • Smallfry does not require an Orca Systems account.
  • Your repositories, plans, and work history are stored on your Mac. We do not upload your source code or project data to our servers.
  • You sign in to coding-agent providers (such as Codex, Claude Code, or Cursor) and to GitHub directly with those services. We do not collect or store those credentials.
  • When you use a coding agent or GitHub through Smallfry, the content you send, such as prompts, code, and diffs, goes to that provider and is governed by its privacy policy.

Tusk

  • Tusk does not require an Orca Systems account.
  • Games you import, along with your saves and settings, are stored on your Mac. Tusk does not upload your game library or saves to us.
  • Tusk does not sell games. Purchases you make from a store such as GOG are handled by that store under its own policy.

App Store

If you get one of our apps from the App Store, Apple handles the purchase and download. Apple provides developers with aggregated sales and usage reports that do not identify individual users to us.

How we use information

We use the limited information we receive to respond to support requests, fulfill privacy requests, and meet legal obligations. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or tracking, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

Third parties

Depending on how you use our products, the following parties may process information:

  • GitHub hosts this website. If you connect GitHub in Smallfry, it also processes your repository data.
  • Apple distributes our apps through the App Store.
  • Coding-agent providers you choose to use with Smallfry.
  • Game stores such as GOG, where you buy games that you later import into Tusk.

Any third party we use to provide our services is required to protect your information in a manner consistent with this policy. Services you connect yourself are governed by their own privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

We keep support email only as long as needed to resolve your request, unless we must keep it longer for legal, tax, or accounting reasons.

Because Smallfry and Tusk store your data on your Mac, you can delete it at any time by deleting the app and its data from your Mac, including its folder in ~/Library/Application Support.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete any personal information we hold about you, or withdraw consent to further contact, by emailing admin@orcasystems.dev. We will respond within 30 days. For data held by a service you connected, such as GitHub or a coding-agent provider, contact that service directly. We’re happy to help you figure out which one.

Children

Our website and apps are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If our practices change, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect. If we ever add optional features that collect new information, such as crash reports, we will describe them here first.
